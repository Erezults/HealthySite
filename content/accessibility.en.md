# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** 14 July 2026

Healthy Circle is committed to making **healthy.erezults.com** usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. This statement covers the marketing website only — for accessibility inside the iOS/macOS app itself, contact us using the details below.

## 2. Conformance standard

This site is designed and built to **partially conform with Israeli Standard 5568 (level AA)**, based on WCAG 2.1 level AA. "Partially conforms" means some parts of the content have not yet been fully tested against every success criterion.

---

## 3. What we've done

- An **accessibility widget** is available on every page (the floating button in the corner of the screen) — it lets you increase or decrease text size, switch to a high-contrast color mode, and stop non-essential animations.
- Descriptive **alt text** on meaningful images, and empty alt text on purely decorative ones.
- Text and background colors are checked for a minimum **4.5:1 contrast ratio**.
- The site can be operated **fully by keyboard**, including a "skip to main content" link at the very start of the page (press Tab on load to reveal it).
- Interactive elements (menus, accordions, links) use real, focusable HTML controls with visible focus outlines.
- The site respects your operating system's "reduce motion" setting, and the on-site widget lets you turn off animations independently of that setting.
- Full right-to-left (RTL) layout support for Hebrew.

---

## 4. Known limitations

We are not aware of any blocking accessibility issues at this time, but full manual testing with screen readers across all pages is ongoing. If you find something that doesn't work with your assistive technology, please tell us — see the contact details above.

---

## 5. How this statement was prepared

This statement was prepared by the developer through a self-assessment of the site against Israeli Standard 5568 (WCAG 2.1 AA), and is reviewed whenever the site's design changes materially.
